# simple sandwitch making

## Assumptions
- The robot starts with both hands empty.
- The kitchen counter is clear and within reach.
- A loaf of sliced bread sits on the counter, still in its sealed plastic bag.
- A jar of peanut butter and a jar of jelly sit on the counter, lids on.
- A butter knife sits on the counter.
- A plate sits on the counter.
- A trash can is within reach.

## Steps
1. Pick up the bread bag with one hand.
2. Find the sealed end of the bag.
3. Grip the sealed end with both hands.
4. Pull the two sides of the seal apart until the bag opens.
5. Reach into the bag and remove two slices of bread.
6. Place the two slices side by side on the plate.
7. Pick up the peanut butter jar with one hand.
8. Twist the lid counterclockwise until it comes off.
9. Set the lid on the counter.
10. Pick up the butter knife with the free hand.
11. Dip the knife into the peanut butter and scoop a small amount.
12. Spread the peanut butter evenly across the top of the first slice.
13. Set the knife on the counter.
14. Pick up the jelly jar with one hand.
15. Twist the lid counterclockwise until it comes off.
16. Set the lid on the counter.
17. Dip the knife into the jelly and scoop a small amount.
18. Spread the jelly evenly across the top of the second slice.
19. Set the knife on the counter.
20. Pick up the first slice by its edges.
21. Place it, peanut-butter side down, on top of the second slice.
22. Pick up the finished sandwich by its edges.
23. Place it centered on the plate.
24. Twist each lid clockwise onto its jar until snug.
25. Place both jars back on the counter.
26. Place the knife on the counter.
27. Place the empty bread bag in the trash can.

## Done When
The plate holds one sandwich with peanut butter on the bottom slice and jelly on the top slice, both jars are closed and back on the counter, the knife is on the counter, and the empty bread bag is in the trash.
