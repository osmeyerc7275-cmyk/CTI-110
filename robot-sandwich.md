# simple sandwitch making
- Pick up the bread bag with one hand.
- Find the sealed end of the bag.
- Grip the sealed end with both hands.
- Pull the two sides of the seal apart until the bag opens.
- Reach into the bag and remove two slices of bread.
- Place the two slices side by side on the plate.
- Pick up the peanut butter jar with one hand.
- Twist the lid counterclockwise until it comes off.
- Set the lid on the counter.
- Pick up the butter knife with the free hand.
- Dip the knife into the peanut butter and scoop a small amount.
- Spread the peanut butter evenly across the top of the first slice.
- Set the knife on the counter.
- Pick up the jelly jar with one hand.
- Twist the lid counterclockwise until it comes off.
- Set the lid on the counter.
- Dip the knife into the jelly and scoop a small amount.
- Spread the jelly evenly across the top of the second slice.
- Set the knife on the counter.
- Pick up the first slice by its edges.
- Place it, peanut-butter side down, on top of the second slice.
- Pick up the finished sandwich by its edges.
- Place it centered on the plate.
- Twist each lid clockwise onto its jar until snug.
- Place both jars back on the counter.
- Place the knife on the counter.
- Place the empty bread bag in the trash can.
